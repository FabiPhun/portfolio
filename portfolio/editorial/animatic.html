Saving Christmas (2024)

All rights go to ©FabianF.

A friend of mine is from Ukraine; however, as the war in Ukraine continues to this day, she has not been able to visit any Christmas markets ever since. As this is her favorite activity around Christmas, I decided to create an animatic in order to cheer her up.

Development Timeline

Thumbnail Board

I jumped in with a basic idea in mind and went right to thumbnailing the whole animatic. The idea is to lay out some of the actions and shots I was imagining.

At the time I always joked that a giant robot version of myself would show up at her place to pick her up and lead her to a Christmas market, hence the idea for this animatic.

Concept Art

To create a good design of my friend, the focus was not on recreation but characterisation.

For example; my friend is quite sharp and direct, she likes to work late nights and doesnt' take bullshit lightly. Capturing the directness and sharpness was represented through triangle shapes, her hard working personality was recreated through tired yet focused eyes.

The earrings' were an additional choice as she always wore them whenever we talked and I personally like them a lot.

Within the standard animation pipeline, the order in which I am doing what seems off; however in this case particularly due to time constraint and other factors, the character concept came second.

Rough Cut

To get a better feel for the flow, I used the thumbnails to create a rough cut. Additionally, music and sound were equally as important.

You will notice that the ending of the animatic still represents the thumbnail cut. This was due to scheduling issues on my part, as my mother was suffering from cancer at that time. However, my friend was still quite enthused to see the animatic.
